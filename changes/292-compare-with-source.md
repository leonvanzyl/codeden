- **Compare a fork or hand-off with its source.** The Diff panel's view menu has a **Compare with** section listing the session it was forked or handed off from, the sessions forked or handed off from it, and its sibling forks. Pick one to see how its code differs: `−` lines are the other session's code as of its last turn, `+` lines this session's files as they are now. A legend names both sides and links to the other session. The comparison is read-only, so it offers no Restore and no line comments.
