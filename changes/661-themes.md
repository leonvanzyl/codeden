- **Themes.** Settings → Appearance now offers theme presets beside CodeDen Dark and CodeDen Light, which stay the default and unchanged: High Contrast Dark (readable in bright rooms), High Contrast Light, Hearth (warm dark) and Dusk (dim dark). Every preset meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Pick one theme for dark mode and one for light mode; System switches between them with your OS.
- **Custom themes.** Start from any preset and change the background, text, muted text, accent, border and status colours, with a live preview, a contrast check and reset to the preset. The command palette switches themes too. Your theme is saved with your settings, so the browser and the desktop app share it, and it applies before the page first paints.
