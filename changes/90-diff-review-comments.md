- **Comment on the agent's changes, line by line.** Hover a line in the Diff panel and click **+** (or drag it over several lines) to write a comment under them. Comments collect across files, with a count in the panel, and survive switching tabs. **Send to agent** sends them as one message, each with its file, lines, side (old or new) and the quoted code, right away or queued behind a running turn.
