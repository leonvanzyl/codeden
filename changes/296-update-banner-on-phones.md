- **The update banner fits a phone.** On a narrow screen (or a desktop column squeezed by the sidebar and panel) the banner squeezed its message to one word per line next to its buttons. It now stacks: the message on top at full width, What's new, Update now and Later on a row below and × in the corner. Wide layouts keep the single row.
- **Settings > About fits a phone.** On a narrow card the logo sits above the title instead of in a column of its own, and Update now and Check now wrap together, in line with the text.
