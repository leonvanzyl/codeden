- **Toasts no longer cover the message box on phones and tablets.** While a session's composer is on screen, toasts now sit just above it wherever they would otherwise overlap it: always on phones, where toasts are full width, and on tablets and narrow windows, where the corner toast reached the Send button. Before, toggling Plan or changing permissions raised a toast over the whole toolbar, so the next tap landed on the toast. The sticky **Approval needed** and **Question from an agent** toasts could also hide Send and Stop until opened. Toasts follow the box as a longer message makes it grow, and as opening or closing the right panel or the terminal drawer moves it. Wide desktop windows and pages without a composer keep toasts in the corner as before.
