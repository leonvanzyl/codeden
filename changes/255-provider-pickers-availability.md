- **Provider choices say which providers can run.** The hand-off dialog now starts on another provider that is signed in, and a provider that isn't installed or signed in shows why, the command that fixes it and a **Check again** button, instead of a **Continue** that starts a session that fails. While the brief is being written, the dialog says so. In a factory's Agents settings, the builder and reviewer provider lists mark providers that can't run, for example "Codex (not signed in)", and choosing one says what happens until it can (builders move to another provider; reviews follow the fallback you set).
