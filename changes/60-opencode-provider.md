- **OpenCode is a third agent provider.** Pick OpenCode for any session (agents, PMs, the orchestrator) and any of the models it's connected to, listed as `provider/model` (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, local models and more) with each model's effort levels. Sessions get the same transcript as the other providers: your messages and briefs, its messages, reasoning, commands, file edits with diffs, searches, MCP tools, todos and subagents in their own threads. Approvals follow your permission level (Read-only to Full) through CodeDen's own risk checks, questions show as question cards, and you can steer, interrupt and resume after a restart. CodeDen's tools reach OpenCode sessions over MCP, and the cost OpenCode reports goes into usage reports (`anthropic/` and `openai/` models are priced from the bundled table when it reports none). Needs OpenCode 2.2 or newer; the setup checklist and Settings › Providers detect it, and **Sign in** runs `opencode auth login`. Each session runs its own private OpenCode server, and your OpenCode config, plugins and credentials are used as they are. CodeDen's sessions are archived in OpenCode's own session list, so they don't clutter it. When OpenCode runs the utility model (titles, follow-ups, read-aloud summaries) without a model picked in Settings, CodeDen uses the cheapest model from your default model's provider.
