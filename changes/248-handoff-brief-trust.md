- **Handing off factory or triage work keeps its trust rules.** The brief of a hand-off used to relay the original session's first message, often a factory or triage brief quoting a GitHub issue, as CodeDen's own words, and clipped it at 4,000 characters, which could cut off the rule that issue text is data, not instructions. Messages from the factory, an automation, a project manager or the orchestrator are now quoted under who sent them, the rule sits at the top of the brief whenever the work is on an issue or wasn't started by you, and a hand-off or fork of such work records who started it, so it no longer receives PR feedback that its source wouldn't have.
