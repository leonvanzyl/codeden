- **Search across transcripts.** Find any session by what was said or run in it, archived sessions included. Type three or more characters in the command palette: a **Transcripts** group under the session matches shows the best hits, each with its session, project, role, a highlighted snippet and when it happened. **Search all transcripts** (also **Search transcripts** under Go to) opens a results page you can filter by project. Search covers your prompts, agents' replies and plans, the commands they ran, the files they read and edited, web searches, subagent tasks and results, questions and answers, approvals, notices and errors. It ignores case and accents and matches inside names and paths, so `cart hook` finds `useCartHook`. Opening a hit opens its session scrolled to that moment and highlights it. A collapsed group of steps opens to show the match, and a match inside a subagent's work opens at that subagent's card. Sessions from before this update are indexed the first time CodeDen starts.
