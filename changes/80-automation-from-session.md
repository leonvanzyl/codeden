- **Turn a session into an automation.** Did a task once by hand and it worked? **Make an automation…** in the session's **⋯** menu (or **Make an automation from this session…** in the command palette) opens the New automation editor for its project, filled in from the session: its opening message as the prompt (attachments left out), its provider, model, effort and permissions, and its title as the name. It runs only when you run it until you pick a schedule. Nothing is saved until you press **Save**, and it starts off like every new automation.
